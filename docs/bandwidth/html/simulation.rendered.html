04AN ENVIRONMENT YOU CAN INSPECT

Computed consequences.
A basis you can examine.

NOMOS is designed to represent the business, its instruments and its legal environment as an executable model. Proposed changes can be checked against formalised conditions, and their effects computed from stated inputs.

Within that model, the same inputs produce the same computed effects. Lean provides the foundation for checkable reasoning. Sources, assumptions and unmodelled questions stay visible alongside the result.

Formal checks establish what follows within the represented rules and facts. Forecasts, unresolved interpretations and real-world outcomes remain explicit assumptions or open questions.
