# RILIN — "ROOM" (106.5 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · 2.39:1 opening to 16:9)

**Idea.** The Rilin mark is a row of pillars, so the film is built of pillars. The work around the work towers over one person as monoliths of stacked paper. Rilin's agents take it away to its owners, the hall opens, and light comes in through a window shaped exactly like the Rilin mark.
